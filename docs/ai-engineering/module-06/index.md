# Module 6 — ML Fundamentals & Statistics

You can't engineer AI systems well without understanding the mathematical engine underneath. You don't need a PhD — but you do need to know why a model is "confident" about a wrong answer, why your training set being too small causes specific problems, and what the numbers mean when you evaluate model quality.

This module builds the foundation: statistics, linear algebra, ML algorithms, and optimization — taught as concepts an engineer needs to reason about AI systems, not as formulas to memorize.
