# Module 4 — RAG, The Bridge

The agent knows how to reason. It doesn't know anything about your business.

TaskFlow's agent could look up a ticket by ID, but it couldn't answer "what's our refund policy for annual plans?" — that lives in a policy document nobody exposed as a tool. Retrieval is how you close that gap.

This module is deliberately short. Retrieval is a large enough subject to have its own track in this handbook, and duplicating it here would guarantee the two copies drift apart.
