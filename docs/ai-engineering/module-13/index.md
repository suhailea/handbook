# Module 13 — AI Business Strategy

TaskFlow shipped. It works. Now leadership asks: "What should we build next? Should we fine-tune a model? Can we use AI to cut support costs by 40%? Should we build this ourselves or buy a vendor solution?"

These are not engineering questions. They're strategy questions — and senior AI engineers are expected to answer them.

This module is where you shift from *building AI systems* to *reasoning about which AI systems are worth building, why, and how to measure whether they worked*.

## Why this module exists

The gap between a mid-level and senior AI engineer isn't just technical depth. It's the ability to walk into a room with product managers and executives and answer:

- "Is this the right problem to solve with AI?"
- "Why AI instead of a rule-based system?"
- "How do we know when it's working?"
- "What does this cost, and what's the return?"
- "What are the risks if it goes wrong?"

If you can only answer technical questions about the implementation, you're an implementer. If you can also answer the strategic questions, you're an architect.
